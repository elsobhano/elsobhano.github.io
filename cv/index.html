Summary PhD researcher at the Centre for Vision, Speech and Signal Processing (CVSSP), University of Surrey, supervised by Prof. Richard Bowden. My thesis is on sign language recognition and translation, with a focus on gloss-free translation, motion-level knowledge transfer across sign languages, and transferable sign representations. I work on vision-language models and multimodal LLMs for sign language translation. First author at ECCV 2026, BMVC 2025 and ACM IVA 2025, co-author of an evaluation study in CVIU, and first author of a further preprint under review. Keywords: vision-language models, multimodal LLMs, video understanding, sign language translation, contrastive representation learning, cross-lingual transfer.

Work

  • 2024.07 - Present

    Guildford, England

    Research Assistant
    Centre for Vision, Speech and Signal Processing (CVSSP), University of Surrey
    Research on gloss-free sign language translation with vision-language models and multimodal LLMs, under the supervision of Prof. Richard Bowden.
    • Own the full research stack for gloss-free sign language translation: data pipelines, pre-training of video-language models on large-scale datasets, multi-GPU training, and evaluation across multiple sign language corpora.
    • Devised a cross-language transfer method that lets a single model serve multiple sign languages, removing the per-language retraining that dataset-specific pipelines require.
    • Built a VideoLLM-based translation framework that generates fine-grained, temporally-aware descriptions of hand motion and contrastively aligns them with video features during pre-training.
    • Built a reproducible evaluation protocol that exposed systematic bias in previously reported gloss-free translation results, re-establishing a reliable baseline for the field.
  • 2023.04 - 2024.04
    Research Assistant
    Electronic Research Institute (ERI) at Sharif University of Technology
    Conducted research and development on machine learning and speech processing systems, focusing on dataset creation and model development for speech processing tasks.
    • Covered dataset creation, model development, and multi-GPU training on downstream speech datasets.
    • Built a speech enhancement front-end for an ASR pipeline, trained so enhanced-speech embeddings match their clean-speech targets under environmental noise and reverberation; exported to ONNX for low-resource inference.
    • Designed a frame-level voice activity detector (CNN/RNN/FNN) to cut ASR compute and error, reaching higher accuracy than contemporary models with fewer parameters and lower CPU runtime.
